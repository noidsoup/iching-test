# Web synthesis — Yijing traveling West

Notes distilled from **Cairn** / *Monde chinois* style analyses of **translation–patronage–celebrity** networks and from **Hugh Urban**-style histories of **American occulture** (counterculture + occult bricolage).

## Key points

- The Wilhelm edition’s authority rested not only on sinological merit but on **institutional patronage** (Bollingen, Princeton) and **psychological legitimation** (Jung).
- Countercultural adoption emphasized **coin methods**, **synchronicity**, and **anti-establishment chance**—uses **orthogonal** to Qing evidential scholarship (考證) yet parasitic on the same book object.
- **Philip K. Dick**, **Bob Dylan**, and **George Harrison** appear in network diagrams as **amplifiers**, not as exegetes; their role is **visibility**, not textual fidelity.

## Detailed notes

For strict philology of the Zhou yi, Western counterculture reception is **source noise**; for **cultural history**, it is **primary material** documenting how hexagrams became **global pop symbols**.
