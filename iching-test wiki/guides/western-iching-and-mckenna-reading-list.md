# Reading list — Western counterculture I Ching & McKenna

## Prerequisites

Read [[western-iching-counterculture-1960s-onward]] then [[mckenna-novelty-theory-and-timewave-zero]] before primary McKenna texts so you can separate **sinology** from **psychedelic hermeneutics**.

## Primary McKenna (I Ching–adjacent)

1. Terence & Dennis McKenna — *The Invisible Landscape: Mind, Hallucinogens, and the I Ching* (1975; reprints). Technical + narrative origin.
2. Terence McKenna — *True Hallucinations* (1989/1993). Expedition memoir framing Timewave’s mythic origin.
3. Terence McKenna — “Where Did the Timewave Come From?” (essay; levity.com/eschaton mirror). Step-by-step public derivation sketch.

## Software / mathematics lineage

- Peter Meyer — **Fractal Time** / Timewave Zero software history and **four number sets** articles on `fractal-timewave.com`.
- Matthew Watkins — *Autopsy for a Mathematical Hallucination?* (John Walker mirror on fourmilab.ch).

## Critical / contextual secondary

- Gyrus (Dreamflesh) — “The End of the River” (Timewave, apocalyptic linearity critique).
- Hugh Urban–adjacent occulture histories for **I Ching in 1960s America** (counterculture + occult bricolage).
- Academic “traveling classic” articles on **Wilhelm–Jung–Bollingen** networks (see [[web-western-yijing-networks-synthesis]]).
