# The Invisible Landscape — *Mind, Hallucinogens, and the I Ching*

Co-authored **Terence and Dennis McKenna** (Seabury, **1975**; later Harper reprints). The work binds **Amazon expedition narrative**, **pharmacology speculation**, and an **appendix-style exposition** of **novelty theory** grounded in manipulations of the **King Wen sequence**.

## Key details

- Contains early **tables of 384 values** (64 × 6 lines) credited to assistants **Royce Kelley** and **Leon Taylor** (per Peter Meyer’s later software history).
- Functions as the **canonical textual witness** for Timewave’s claimed derivation—later software manuals exposed steps not fully specified in prose.
