# Edward L. Shaughnessy

American **sinologist** specializing in **Western Zhou** texts and **manuscript studies**. Widely cited for **dating arguments** about the Zhou yi’s stabilization and for **editions/translations** of newly excavated Changes manuscripts (including **Mawangdui** and **Shanghai Museum** Zhou yi materials).
