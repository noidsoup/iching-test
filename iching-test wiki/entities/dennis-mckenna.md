# Dennis McKenna

**Pharmacologist** and **ethnopharmacologist**; Terence’s younger brother and co-author of *The Invisible Landscape*. Dennis’s published accounts emphasize **La Chorrera** as a joint **experimental** episode (tryptamine “hypercarbolation” hypotheses, vocal techniques) whose outcomes Terence later **reframed** into I Ching–based time theory.
