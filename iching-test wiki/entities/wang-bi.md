# Wang Bi

**Wang Bi** (226–249 CE) wrote the most influential **Three Kingdoms–era** commentary on the Changes, **subordinating Han image–number exegesis** to a **philosophical reading** grounded in the **Ten Wings**. His edition became the **Tang imperial standard** via Kong Yingda’s subcommentary and shaped East Asian literati reception for centuries.
