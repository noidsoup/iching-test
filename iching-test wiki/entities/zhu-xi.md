# Zhu Xi

Song **Neo-Confucian** synthesizer who argued the Yijing should be read as **divination-first** yet ethically formative. His reconstruction of **yarrow stalk** procedure from **Great Commentary** passages became the **standard East Asian practice** alongside later coin methods.
