# Mawangdui silk Yijing manuscripts

**Mawangdui** tomb **3** (Changsha, Hunan) yielded silk manuscripts of the Changes sealed **168 BCE**. The find includes **variant character forms**, **partial Wing traditions**, and a **different hexagram order** (eight groups by **upper trigram**) compared to the received **King Wen sequence**. Many **hexagram names** differ phonographically from received titles while pointing to the same figures.

## Key details

- Tomb occupant context: **Li Cang** family burial assemblage.
- Scholarly editions: **Edward Shaughnessy** translation and numerous Chinese archaeological reports.
