# Terence McKenna

American **lecturer**, **ethnobotanist**, and **psychedelic theorist** best known publicly for **psilocybin advocacy**, the **“stoned ape”** hypothesis (*Food of the Gods*), and **novelty theory** / **Timewave Zero**—a speculative system that re-reads the **King Wen sequence** of the I Ching as the seed of a **fractal time map**. He co-authored the foundational statement with his brother **Dennis** in *The Invisible Landscape* (1975) and narrated the La Chorrera backstory in *True Hallucinations* (1989/1993).

## How it's used in this project

The **being** app’s hexagram JSON is **not** derived from McKenna’s mathematics; this page situates **popular Western I Ching reception** adjacent to **sinological** pages in the vault.

## Key details

- **La Chorrera**, Colombia (**1971**): psilocybin-heavy expedition; McKenna described visionary **“voice of the Logos”** experiences that he linked to later I Ching structural work.
- **Software collaborator**: **Peter Meyer** implemented and maintained Timewave code (MS-DOS through **Fractal Time**, 1999).
- **Other public corpus** (less I Ching–centric but culturally paired): *Food of the Gods* (1992) on **psilocybin** and human history; *The Archaic Revival* (1991) collects talks on **neo-shamanism** and technoculture.
- **Death**: 2000; he did **not** live to the 2012 calendar node.
