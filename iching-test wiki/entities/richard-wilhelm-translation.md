# Richard Wilhelm / Cary Baynes translation

**Richard Wilhelm**’s German **I Ching** (1924), translated into English by **Cary Baynes** (1950, Princeton Bollingen), became the **dominant 20th-century English** gateway—interleaving wings and judgments in a **study edition** format rather than isolating Zhou yi like some philological translations (Legge, Rutt). **Carl Jung**’s foreword amplified its role in **psychology** and **counterculture** oracular practice.
