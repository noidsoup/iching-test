# Shanghai Museum Chu bamboo Zhou yi

A **Warring States** bamboo manuscript of the **Zhou yi** among the **Shanghai Museum** purchased **Chu bamboo-slip corpus** (1994). **~300 BCE** copying is supported by paleography and published radiocarbon ranges. The strips preserve roughly **a third** of the received classic’s physical extent but demonstrate **broad textual agreement** with minor **pre-standardization** variation.
