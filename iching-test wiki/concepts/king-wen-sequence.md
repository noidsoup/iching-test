# King Wen hexagram sequence

The **King Wen sequence** is the canonical ordering of the sixty-four hexagrams in transmitted **Yijing** editions—named for the Zhou culture hero **King Wen**. The sequence **pairs** most hexagrams with their **180° rotations**; the eight **self-inverse** diagrams pair by **yin–yang complement** instead. The **rationale for pair order** and global progression remains **obscure**; scholars debate whether the pattern encodes musicology, politics, or accumulated editorial habit.

Archaeology shows **other orders** were used (notably **Mawangdui**’s upper-trigram grouping), while some evidence suggests **King Wen order** may already appear on **Western Zhou** artifacts—interpretations remain cautious.

## Application here

The app’s JSON uses **six-bit binary keys** (e.g. `111111`) and picks randomly; that is **not** the same as King Wen numbering unless you explicitly map keys to the received table.
