# Yixue — image–number vs. meaning–principle

**Yi learning** (易學 *Yìxué*) names the totality of technical and philosophical traditions orbiting the Changes. Two poles recur:

- **Image–number** (象數): correlative cosmology, **najia** 納甲, **gua qi** 卦氣, **wind–rain** numerologies, Han **New Text** eclecticism, and later Daoist inner-alchemy resonances.
- **Meaning–principle** (義理): moral psychology, statecraft, and metaphysics read directly from wings and judgments—**Old Text** moralism, then **Wang Bi**’s sublation of Han numerology, then **Cheng–Zhu** Neo-Confucian syntheses.

**Zhu Xi** argued the classic remained **fundamentally divinatory** yet instrumental for **self-cultivation**—a position that re-anchors practice after Wang Bi’s partial demystification of Han images.
