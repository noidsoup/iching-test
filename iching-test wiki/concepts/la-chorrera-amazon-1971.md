# La Chorrera expedition (1971)

**La Chorrera** (Amazonian Colombia) names the village region where **Terence and Dennis McKenna** and companions pursued a **self-designed psychonautic experiment** in **March–April 1971** (public chronology often cites **4 March 1971** as departure). The brothers combined **high-dose Psilocybe cubensis** experiences, **harmine**-related pharmacology speculation, and **vocal-toning** protocols aimed at “bonding” tryptamine chemistry to neural tissue—goals framed in alchemical and science-fiction diction.

Terence later narrated **voice phenomena** (“**Logos**,” “**mushroom**”) and **geometric downloads** that he retroactively connected to **structural study of the I Ching’s King Wen ordering**—the mythopoetic origin story for **novelty theory**.

## Epistemic note

Historians of religion classify such accounts as **emic narratives** of altered states; they are **not** independent evidence for claims about **Zhou dynasty** divination practice.
