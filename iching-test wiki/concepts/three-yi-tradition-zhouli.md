# Three Yi systems in Rites of Zhou

The **Rites of Zhou** (周禮) names three royal divination canons—**Lianshan** 連山, **Guicang** 歸藏, and **Zhouyi** 周易—each ostensibly organized with **eight trigrams** and **sixty-four hexagrams**. Only the **Zhouyi** line survived intact into the imperial tradition; **Guicang** fragments resurfaced at **Wangjiatai** (Qin tomb texts) and **Lianshan** remains largely **legendary** in received form.
