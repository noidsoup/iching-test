# Zhou yi versus Yijing canon

The **Zhou yi** (周易) is the ancient **divination core**: sixty-four hexagram figures with names, hexagram statements, and line statements in **Early Old Chinese**. The **Yijing / I Ching** (易經) is the **imperial canon name** for the Zhou yi **after** it was paired with the **Ten Wings** commentaries and elevated among the **Five Classics** under Han Wudi (136 BCE). In modern scholarship the distinction marks **two strata of language**, **two functions** (oracle vs. cosmological–ethical encyclopedia), and **two reception histories** (pre-canonical witnesses vs. stone-classics standard).
