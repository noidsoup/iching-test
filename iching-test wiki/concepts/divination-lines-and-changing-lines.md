# Divination mechanics — lines, numbers, and changing hexagrams

Classical cleromancy produces six draws mapping to **six lines**. Broken lines conventionally encode **6** (old yin) and **8** (young yin); solid lines encode **7** (young yang) and **9** (old yang). **Changing lines** (6 and 9) flip to generate a **secondary hexagram**; commentaries then weigh **judgment** vs. **line texts** depending on period and school.

**Yarrow** protocols are reconstructed from the **Great Commentary** description and stabilized in **Zhu Xi**’s Song-dynasty synthesis. **Three-coin** methods simplify entropy gathering and became widespread by the **Tang** and after.
