# Timewave mathematics — data sets, half-twist, critiques

Timewave construction is a **two-stage pipeline**:

1. **Vectorization**: derive **384 integers** from the **King Wen ordering** by encoding **line diffs** between successive hexagrams (and related skew/parallelism heuristics documented in *The Invisible Landscape* appendices). Early tables were computed manually / by assistants **Royce Kelley** and **Leon Taylor** (1974-era, per Meyer’s software history).
2. **Fractal transform**: map those primitives through a **self-similar rescaling** procedure (Meyer’s **MS-DOS** programs **WEN_GRPH**, **TimeExplorer**, culminating in **Fractal Time**, 1999) to output **novelty vs. time** graphs at multiple scales (powers of 64).

## The “half-twist” and the Watkins Objection

When Meyer reverse-engineered the published algorithm against McKenna’s prose description (**1994**), he found a **sign-flip routine** applied to half of an intermediate array—dubbed the **“mysterious half-twist”** in software documentation because **no first-principles justification** appeared in the 1975 book.

**Matthew Watkins** formalized the pipeline as a **single formula** and argued the **(-1)^k-style** injections are **ad hoc**: removing them yields a **different 384-vector** (“**Watkins**” set) and a visibly different wave. McKenna **rebranded** the correction discourse as the **“Watkins Objection”** and sometimes treated **de-twisted** waves as alternate **maps of habit** rather than novelty.

## Variant number sets (Meyer’s taxonomy)

Meyer catalogs **at least four** published 384-point families—**Kelley** (original appendix), **Watkins** (Kelley minus half-twist per Meyer’s conformance patch), **Sheliak**, and **Huang Ti**—reflecting **forking community mathematics** after Watkins and **John Sheliak**’s vector-style “clarification.” Meyer’s site notes **Sheliak** and **Huang Ti** constructions as **more controversial** even within novelty-theory insider debates.

## Eschatological calibration

McKenna chose **2012-12-21** as the **global zero** after **initial numerical experiments** (e.g. **Hiroshima** date anchoring stories in secondary literature); **Maya** calendar alignment was **retrofitted** as confirmatory myth—historians treat this as **bricolage**, not independent convergence testing.

## Critical essays

Philosophical critiques (e.g. **Gyrus** / Dreamflesh “End of the River”) attack **residual linear apocalypticism** inside a theory marketed as **fractal non-linearity**. **Skeptical mathematicians** treat Timewave as **unfalsifiable pattern-matching** post-hoc onto big history.
