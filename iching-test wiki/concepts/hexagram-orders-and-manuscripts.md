# Hexagram orders and manuscript witnesses

Beyond the received **King Wen sequence**, ancient China transmitted the sixty-four figures in **alternate linear orders**. The **Mawangdui** silk Yijing (Western Han, tomb sealed 168 BCE) groups hexagrams into **eight blocks** sharing the **upper trigram**—a “finding order” that may reflect **Jing Fang**–style eight-palace logic as later theorized, not necessarily the Zhou yi’s earliest reading practice.

**Shanghai Museum** Chu bamboo **Zhou yi** (~300 BCE) confirms the **core text** was already **largely stable** while allowing **small graphic and lexical drift** across regions.
