# Matthew Watkins

British **mathematician** who, in the **1990s**, reverse-engineered Meyer’s published **C code** into a **closed formula** for the Timewave’s **384 data points** and argued that the **“half-twist”** step lacks stated justification—**McKenna** dubbed this the **“Watkins Objection.”** Watkins’s long-form critique circulated with **John Walker**’s hosting (*Autopsy for a Mathematical Hallucination?*) and **Reality Sandwich** republications.
